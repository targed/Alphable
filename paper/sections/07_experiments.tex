\section{Empirical Evaluation and Statistical Rigor}
\label{sec:experiments}

To rigorously test whether \algo's observed performance edge represents a statistically valid algorithmic advantage or an artifact of sample variance and random tile draws, we conduct extensive paired duplicate tournaments.

\subsection{Paired Duplicate Tournament Methodology}

In standard tournament Scrabble, tile distribution variance is large: drawing blank tiles ($?$) or high-scoring letters ($S$) exerts substantial influence over individual game scores. To isolate pure algorithmic decision quality from tile luck, we employ the \textbf{Paired Duplicate Tournament} format with Common Random Numbers (CRN) \citep{olaughlin2006quackle}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Each match consists of a duplicate pair comprising two games (Game A and Game B) played on identical bag sequences $\sigma \sim \mathrm{Permutations}(100)$.
    \item In Game A, Player 1 moves first (Seat 1) and Player 2 moves second (Seat 2).
    \item In Game B, the seats are swapped: Player 2 plays first with the identical opening rack, and Player 1 plays second.
    \item The pair score differential is defined as:
    \begin{equation}
        \Delta_{\mathrm{pair}} = \frac{(S_{1, A} - S_{2, A}) + (S_{1, B} - S_{2, B})}{2}
    \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}
In Appendix~\ref{app:proof_duplicate}, we prove that $\E[\Delta_{\mathrm{pair}}] = \mu_1 - \mu_2$, demonstrating that seat advantage and tile luck cancel out algebraically.

\subsection{Game-Phase Sensitivity Breakdown}

Where does \algo\ extract its competitive equity? We instrumented match telemetry across four distinct game phases:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Opening:} Turns 1 to 5.
    \item \textbf{Midgame:} Turn $>5$ and remaining bag $|\bag| > 14$.
    \item \textbf{Pre-Endgame:} Remaining bag $1 \le |\bag| \le 14$.
    \item \textbf{Endgame:} Remaining bag $|\bag| = 0$.
\end{enumerate}

As shown in Table~\ref{tab:phase_breakdown}, \algo's advantage is not an artifact of early point greed. In fact, \algo\ willingly accepts minor early-game deficits ($-4.00$ pts over opening and midgame turns) to preserve rack balance and restrict open hook lanes. It then unleashes an insurmountable \textbf{$+17.67$ point compound late-game advantage} through pre-endgame bag parity control ($+7.60$ pts) and complete-information alpha-beta minimax lookahead ($+10.07$ pts).
