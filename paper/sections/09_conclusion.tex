\section{Conclusion and Future Directions}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we introduced \algo, an open-source Scrabble engine designed to bridge the gap between high-speed compiled candidate generation and deep imperfect-information valuation. By combining compiled C move generation with deep cross-attention value networks, Bayesian negative-evidence belief tracking, and Belief-Augmented Monte Carlo Search (\bams), \algo\ resolves both the decoupled valuation of static heuristics and the strategy fusion flaw of unconstrained PIMC.

In dual-tier duplicate tournament benchmarks comprising 20,000 total games, \algo\ established two foundational empirical findings. Against 1-ply static heuristic \magpie, 2-ply \bams\ achieved a $+7.06 \pm 0.81$ point net spread per game ($p = 2.90 \times 10^{-18}$, $t = 8.75$) across 10,000 games, driven by an insurmountable $+17.67$ point compound advantage in pre-endgame and endgame play. Against Maximally Tuned Base \magpie\ equipped with compiled PIMC simulation, positional pattern terms, and exact endgame solvers, \algo\ Hybrid PIMC achieved a $+4.15$ point net spread per game ($95\%$ CI: $[+2.68, +5.61]$, total cumulative net spread of $+41,474$ points, $p = 2.83 \times 10^{-8}$, paired $t = 5.5517$) across 10,000 games (5,000 duplicate pairs) executed on the Missouri S\&T supercomputer cluster, exceeding the 5.5-sigma empirical discovery threshold.

Our critical audit demonstrated that small-scale matches (such as 40-game probes) suffer from high standard error ($\mathrm{SEM} \approx 23.6$ pts/pair) that can yield severely deceptive swings, underscoring the absolute necessity of large-sample supercomputer evaluations in imperfect-information games. Furthermore, while pure two-ply neural search is constrained in search depth compared to unconstrained simulation rollouts at $\sim 98,000$ rollouts per second, fusing high-throughput simulation with deep neural re-ranking resolves the trade-off, outperforming either paradigm operating in isolation.

Future work includes compiling neural tensor operations directly into native C inference runtimes (such as ONNX Runtime or TensorRT) to evaluate deep neural networks at full simulation speed. To support reproducible research in imperfect-information game AI, the complete \algo\ codebase, neural model weights, SLURM orchestration scripts, and full 10,000-game match logs are available under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3) at \url{https://github.com/targed/Alphable}.
