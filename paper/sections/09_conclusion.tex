\section{Conclusion and Future Directions}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we introduced \algo, an open-source Scrabble engine designed to bridge the gap between high-speed compiled candidate generation and deep imperfect-information valuation. By combining C move generation with deep cross-attention value networks, Bayesian negative-evidence belief tracking, and Belief-Augmented Monte Carlo Search (\bams), \algo\ addresses the decoupled valuation of static heuristics and the strategy fusion flaw of unconstrained PIMC.

In dual-tier duplicate tournament benchmarks, \algo\ established two clear findings:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Against 1-ply static heuristic \magpie, 2-ply \bams\ achieved a $+7.06 \pm 0.81$ point net spread per game ($p = 2.90 \times 10^{-18}$, $t = 8.75$) across 10,000 games, driven by a $+17.67$ point compound advantage in pre-endgame and endgame play.
    \item Against Maximally Tuned Base \magpie\ equipped with compiled PIMC simulation, positional pattern terms, and exact endgame solvers, \algo\ Hybrid PIMC achieved a $+4.15$ point net spread per game ($95\%$ CI: $[+2.68, +5.61]$, total net $+41,474$ points, $p = 2.83 \times 10^{-8}$, paired $t = 5.5517$) across 10,000 games (5,000 duplicate pairs) executed on the Missouri S\&T HPC cluster. The result exceeds the 5.5-sigma discovery threshold.
\end{enumerate}

Our critical audit showed that small-sample matches (such as 40-game probes) suffer from high standard error ($\mathrm{SEM} \approx 23.6$ pts/pair) that can yield misleading swings. We also showed that while pure 2-ply neural search is constrained in search depth compared to unconstrained simulation rollouts at $\sim 98,000$ rollouts/second, fusing high-throughput simulation with neural re-ranking resolves the trade-off.

Future work includes compiling neural tensor operations directly into native C inference runtimes (e.g., via ONNX Runtime or TensorRT) to evaluate deep neural networks at full simulation speed. To support reproducible research in imperfect-information game AI, the complete \algo\ codebase, neural model weights, SLURM orchestration scripts, and full 10,000-game match logs are available under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3) at \url{https://github.com/Alphable/Alphable}.
